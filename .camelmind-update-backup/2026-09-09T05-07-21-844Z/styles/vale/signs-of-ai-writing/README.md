# Signs of AI Writing

## About

Custom [Vale](https://github.com/errata-ai/vale/) style that flags leftover artifacts and tells of unedited AI-generated prose — ChatGPT citation markers, knowledge-cutoff disclaimers, hedged superlatives, stock "symbolic" phrasing ("stands as a testament to..."), and similar patterns.

Vendored from [circleci/circleci-docs](https://github.com/circleci/circleci-docs/tree/main/styles/signs-of-ai-writing), originally from [ammil-industries/vale-signs-of-ai-writing](https://github.com/ammil-industries/vale-signs-of-ai-writing) (CC-BY-SA-4.0).
